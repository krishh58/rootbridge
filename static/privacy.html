Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data RootBridge collects, how we use it, and your rights over it.

1. Information We Collect

Account data: Your email address and hashed password when you register.

Tree and genealogy data: Names, dates, places, and relationships you enter for persons in your family tree.

Uploaded documents: Files you upload for AI analysis (images, PDFs, text). These are stored in our database and processed by an AI model to extract genealogy facts.

Search data: The queries sent to public records sources (WikiTree, Chronicling America, DPLA, NARA) and the results returned. These are stored against your tree.

Usage data: Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps, pages visited) used for security and debugging. Not sold or shared.

Payment data: Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status — we never see or store your card number.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the genealogy research service
  • To match your ancestors with other users researching the same family lines (opt-in, controlled by your Discovery setting)
  • To send account emails: verification, password reset, security notifications
  • To send match notifications when another user researches the same ancestor (if you have not disabled this)
  • To process subscription payments and top-ups

3. AI Processing

Documents you upload and search queries are sent to an AI service (via OpenRouter) for analysis and summarization. Document contents are transmitted securely over HTTPS and are not used to train AI models. Summaries are stored in your account.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • OpenRouter / Anthropic — document and query text for AI analysis (subject to their privacy policies)
  • Public records APIs — name and date queries to WikiTree, DPLA, NARA, Chronicling America
  • Railway — our hosting provider (stores encrypted database backups)

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

5. Data About Other People

When you enter information about relatives — including living persons — you take responsibility for having appropriate basis to do so. Genealogy data about deceased individuals is generally considered public interest research. Data about living relatives is more sensitive and should be kept in private trees.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes — we cannot read them. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We apply rate limiting to prevent brute-force attacks. Optional two-factor authentication is available for all accounts.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your data via account settings
  • Correction — update your tree and account data at any time
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data from account settings
  • Portability — export your family tree as a GEDCOM file at any time
  • Opt-out — disable discovery matching in account settings

To exercise these rights, use the account settings page or email support@rootbridge.app.

8. Data Retention

Your account data is retained until you delete your account. Uploaded documents are retained until you delete them or delete your account. Server logs are retained for 90 days.

9. Cookies

We use a single HTTP-only cookie to maintain your login session. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

10. Children

RootBridge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified by email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@rootbridge.app.
